How Texas Flood Check works

During a flash flood, people send their location and answer six questions. Every request is ranked so responders reach the people in the most danger first. Flood history for that spot counts, but it never outranks someone who says they are in danger now.

What it can't do

It is a student prototype, not a real emergency service. Anyone in danger should call 911.
The aerial photos show past floods, not what is happening right now.
The AI's flood check is a second opinion for responders to verify, not a decision.
A river, pond or pool can look like flooding from the air, and the before and after photos are a year apart.
